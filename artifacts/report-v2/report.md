## Interpretation and limits

- Three predefined LIBERO tasks and three fixed initial states; one checkpoint, seed, execution horizon and interruption. No universal ranking or reliability estimate.
- Replay of a full nominal trajectory previously generated by closed-loop SmolVLA on this same initial state. Not a single-shot whole-task VLA plan.
- Clean replay is expected to match the nominal reference by construction; it is a reproducibility control, not independent evidence for equal general policy quality.
- Simulation pauses during synchronous inference. All modes retain the same low-level OSC feedback. Open online inference excludes nominal plan construction; full planning costs are listed separately.
- A reference records all 300 model-generated controls, including after first success, to provide a full common-budget replay plan. Evaluation uses first success and corresponding policy calls.
- Tasks, initial IDs and interruption interval were frozen before the interrupted outcomes; all valid outcomes retained.
- The interruption withholds commands 30–39 inclusive, replaces their Cartesian components with zero, and preserves the previously executed gripper command. Simulation/controller feedback continue. Both modes receive the same intervention; there is no added recovery policy.
- Success is LIBERO's simulator predicate, not valid inference or a controller acknowledgement. A first-attainment success is not a claim of sustained goal stability. Timeouts are not assigned invented completion times. Success-only means have different denominators when success rates differ.
- Three selected initial states per task are too few for a general correlation or universal ordering. We do not search for extra cases just to obtain mixed results.

## Inference and cost

Measured 370 actual chunk calls: median 0.287 s; p95 0.339 s; peak PyTorch GPU allocation 1.39 GiB. Model-loading time and driver/context memory are additional. The model predicts 50 actions and executes ten before fresh inference; this horizon is fixed, not an ablation.

Open-loop replay makes zero online model calls, but obtaining each nominal plan cost 30 VLA calls. See summary.json for every plan's wall/inference cost; zero online calls does not mean free end-to-end planning.
